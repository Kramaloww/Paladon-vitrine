**The everyday copilot that stays at home.**

Paladon brings your finances, investments, news, tasks and alerts together in a single app. Installed on your own machine and backed by a local AI, it keeps your data where it belongs: at home.

🌐 **[paladon.net/en](https://paladon.net/en/)**

## Why Paladon

- **Everything in one place**: net worth, budget, savings goals, stocks, news and tasks.
- **Your data stays home**: self-hosted, reachable only through a private network, never exposed to the Internet.
- **An AI that explains, without making things up**: figures come from tested calculations, and the local AI puts them into words.
- **No AI in the cloud**: the model runs on your machine; nothing is sent to an AI provider.

## Status

Paladon is a personal project and is not available yet. The app is currently in French only.
