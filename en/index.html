Features

Your whole daily life, in a single app

Designed first for personal finance, Paladon now also covers your investments, your news, your organization and your alerts.

Net worth and accounts

Checking accounts, savings accounts, brokerage accounts and life insurance gathered in one place. A snapshot of your net worth is taken every day, and you can follow it by day, week, month or year.

All your banks, without double counting

Connect one or more banks in read-only mode (European PSD2 standard): your checking accounts sync automatically. For now, savings accounts are entered manually, and investments are entered or imported; their prices update on their own. Transfers between your own accounts are detected automatically: they count neither as an expense nor as a second income.

Savings goals

Set an amount to reach, a deadline if you wish, and the accounts that contribute to it. Paladon tracks the progress of each goal, and the copilot can tell you where you stand.

Budget and insights

Monthly summary and savings rate computed for you, subscriptions and direct debits detected, and a cash flow diagram to see at a glance where the money goes.

Custom rules and tags

Categorization rules that combine description, amount and account, and can add a tag. A transaction can be split across several categories, and tags (holidays, renovation…) have their own totals. Correcting a category offers to turn it into a rule.

Taxes (France)

An income tax estimate with the marginal bracket and a step-by-step calculation, a simulator for the French PER retirement plan, and a comparison of French savings products (Livret A, PEA, life insurance, PER), with sources. Based on French tax rules.

Stocks and charts

Stock and ETF prices updated automatically, candlestick charts with moving averages, Bollinger bands, RSI and MACD, drawing tools, and side-by-side comparison of up to four securities.

AI-managed paper portfolio

The AI manages a virtual portfolio: it reads the news, places simulated orders with fees and safeguards, and keeps a log compared with the CAC 40. No real money involved.

Simulated sports betting*

A statistical model (Elo, form, odds) stakes a virtual €1,000 to measure whether it beats the bookmakers. No real bets.

* For now, simulated bets cover tennis only.

Local AI copilot

Ask your questions in plain language. The copilot queries tested functions, explains their results and shows its sources. It runs entirely on your machine.

Proactive summary and alerts

After each import and on the home page, Paladon points out what deserves your attention: a category soaring above your average, idle cash sitting in your checking account, a new subscription, transactions to categorize. These alerts are computed by the code, not by the AI.

Morning briefing

At the time of your choice: the weather, your calendar, today's direct debits, open bets and yesterday's portfolio, combined in a single notification.

Notifications

Via Web Push on your phone or browser, and in your Discord channels: an investment dropping, a price alert, a bank to reconnect… For each one, you choose where it goes and how detailed it is, with or without amounts.

Widget home page

Build your home page: net worth, latest transactions, direct debits, weather, calendar, stocks, bets, news… Every widget can be moved and resized.

News and AI leaderboard In progress

Your feeds sorted into AI, Tech, Dev and World, a world map of topics, and the Arena leaderboard of AI models. Filters by source and keyword are being finalized.

Tasks In progress

Boards for your personal projects: columns, cards and colored labels. The page is being finalized.

Privacy and architecture

Your data stays at home

Paladon is not an online service: it is an app you install on your own machine, and you stay in control of it.

  • Self-hosted

    Paladon installs with Docker on the machine of your choice: a computer, a mini PC or a home server. Backups are encrypted.

  • Private access

    You reach it from your devices through an encrypted private network. No port is open to the Internet.

  • The code calculates, the AI explains

    Every figure comes from a tested function. If an AI-written text contains a made-up number, it is replaced by the code's own summary.

  • No AI in the cloud

    The AI model runs locally with Ollama. None of your data is sent to an online AI.
